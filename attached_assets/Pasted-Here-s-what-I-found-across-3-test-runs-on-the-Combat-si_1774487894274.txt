Here's what I found across 3 test runs on the Combat sim (seed 12345, 30x30, 3 survivors, 8 zombies, 3 nests): [wss-revised.replit](https://wss-revised.replit.app/)

## 1. Zombie Attack Damage in Combat Log — NOT APPEARING

Across all 3 runs, the combat log **never shows zombie attack damage entries**. Survivors take damage (HP bars visibly decrease, survivors die), but the log only contains:
- Kill events: "X killed a zombie"
- Death events: "X has DIED!"
- Ammo warnings: "X is out of ammo!"
- Evacuations: "X evacuated through the portal!"
- Pickups: "X picked up HealthPack (+30 HP)", "X grabbed ammo"
- Objectives: "Objective N activated!"

**Missing:** Something like "Zombie attacked Aria for X damage" — this needs to be added to the combat log so you can tell whether deaths are from zombie attacks, starvation, or other causes.

## 2. Evacuation Sprint-to-Portal — WORKING

In Run 1 (MISSION COMPLETE at T:5,277), all 3 objectives were completed, portal opened, and survivors successfully evacuated. Boone evacuated at T:3,475, Aria at T:3,690, then Cal at T:5,276. The EVAC phase triggered correctly and survivors pathfound to the portal. [wss-revised.replit](https://wss-revised.replit.app/)

One oddity: Aria evacuated at T:3,690 but then somehow died at T:4,817 — this might be a display/ordering bug, or Aria was fighting near the portal and died before fully evacuating. Worth investigating.

## 3. Balance Tuning Observations

- **Run 1:** MISSION COMPLETE, 2/3 survived, T:5,277. Fairly quick and successful.
- **Run 2:** GAME OVER at T:16,213. Only 2/3 objectives, 0 total kills displayed (possible bug — log shows kills happened). All died.
- **Run 3:** GAME OVER at T:35,109. Only 1/3 objectives, 11 total kills. All died.

**Balance issues:**
- High variance between runs (same seed produces different outcomes due to RNG). Run 1 won easily; Runs 2-3 were total wipes.
- Zombie scaling from nests (8→10→12) overwhelms survivors who run out of ammo.
- **"Total kills" counter was 0 in Run 2 despite log showing kills** — likely a bug in the GAME OVER summary.
- Ammo scarcity is the main death driver. Once ammo runs out, survivors just flee until they die.
- Survivors seem to die from attrition without clear combat log feedback (ties back to issue #1).
